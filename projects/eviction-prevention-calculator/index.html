30-day period —
Earned method Allocates each check to the work-period days that overlap the 30-day window.
Why this calculation may differ from pay-frequency monthly income

This worksheet is designed to document income within an exact rolling 30-day period. It counts checks paid in that window or prorates each check by the work-period days overlapping that window, depending on the selected method. The resulting 30-day amount is used as the monthly income figure.

A pay-frequency projection answers a different question: what income is expected in a typical average month. That method uses the average gross check multiplied by annual pay periods and divided by 12:

  • Weekly: average check × 52 ÷ 12
  • Bi-weekly: average check × 26 ÷ 12
  • Semi-monthly: average check × 24 ÷ 12
  • Monthly: average check × 12 ÷ 12

The results can differ because a rolling 30-day period may contain partial work periods, five weekly checks, three bi-weekly checks, overtime, or other irregular pay. Use the method required by the applicable program and document any exception in the calculation notes.
