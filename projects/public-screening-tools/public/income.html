Calculation and audit details

Paystub basis: average all included representative gross checks, multiply by annual pay periods, then convert the annualized result into an average month and an estimated 30-day period.

  • Weekly: average check × 52
  • Bi-weekly: average check × 26
  • Semi-monthly: average check × 24
  • Monthly: average check × 12

Actual prior-30-day basis: count all entered checks with pay dates inside the as-of-date window, plus other income explicitly entered as received during that window.

Older paystubs remain usable for projection. The widget reports their age and warns staff to confirm continued employment or wage changes. Both basis results and the historical earned amount remain in the audit record regardless of the selected primary basis.

AMI has its own income-basis toggle. It can use the annualized 30-day estimate or the actual amount received in the previous 30 days, independently of the highlighted primary result.

Hourly schedule basis: hourly wage × weekly hours × 52 ÷ 12 gives an average month. The 30-day estimate prorates weekly hours across the exact window, respects the employment start date, and substitutes reduced weekly hours for any overlapping reduced-hours dates.
